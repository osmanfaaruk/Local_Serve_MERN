The Problem We're Solving

In a city as vibrant as Chittagong, finding a trustworthy plumber, electrician, or tutor shouldn't be a game of chance. For years, residents have had to rely on fragmented word-of-mouth or unverified social media listings, often leading to inconsistent quality, unpredictable pricing, and genuine safety concerns.

We noticed a community struggling to find reliable assistance, while at the same time, incredibly skilled local professionals lacked a formal platform to showcase their expertise and reach the right clients. This gap created a barrier to growth and a lack of accountability in the local service industry.

LocalServe was born to bridge this gap. We've built a digital ecosystem where quality is vetted, and trust is the foundation of every handshake.

Trust-First Approach

Every provider on our platform undergoes a multi-step verification process. We check backgrounds, portfolios, and reviews to ensure your safety and satisfaction.

Hyper-Local Focus

We don't just serve the city; we serve the neighborhood. Whether you're in Chadgaon or Halishahar, we help you find experts just a few blocks away.

Empowering Pros

We provide local businesses with the digital tools they need to reach more customers, manage their reputation, and grow their income sustainably.
